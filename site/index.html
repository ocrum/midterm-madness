Method

  • Race odds: Kalshi and Polymarket weighted 1 each, PredictIt 0.25.
  • 4,000 simulated elections. Each of the 470 races wins at its market odds; races move together through one national swing, sized so chamber control matches the control markets (Senate 63% vs 63%, House 91% vs 90%).
  • Senate margins: normal curve centered to match each race's win odds; spread fitted to its margin and vote-share markets.
  • Seat totals: race sums blended 50/50 with the seat-count markets.
  • Highest expected score: every answer chosen for the most expected points.
  • Best chance of first: starting from there, answers are swapped one at a time while the chance of finishing first rises.
